# PAPER WASTE SUSTAINABILITY INFORMATION

# Sustainability Information for magazines

## Recycling Guidelines
For proper magazines disposal, separate components before taking to specialized collection points.

## Creative Reuse
Instead of discarding magazines, make kitchen organizers that helps organize your space.

## Environmental Impact
magazines takes several human lifetimes to decompose in landfills, releasing methane gas.

## Disposal Methods
1. check local guidelines before disposal.
2. use specialized recycling service.
3. Remember to avoid contamination with food.
