# ELECTRONIC WASTE SUSTAINABILITY INFORMATION

# Sustainability Information for chargers

## Recycling Guidelines
When recycling chargers, always remove any food residue. Avoid including food residue as it leads to landfill disposal.

## Creative Reuse
Instead of discarding chargers, make functional gadgets that teaches children about sustainability.

## Environmental Impact
chargers production requires thousands of gallons of water, depleting clean air quality.

## Disposal Methods
1. rinse thoroughly before disposal.
2. place in appropriate bin.
3. Remember to keep materials separated.

# Sustainability Information for smartphones

## Recycling Guidelines
Most recycling facilities process smartphones by mechanical separation, requiring proper sorting.

## Creative Reuse
smartphones can be repurposed into gift packaging by weaving patterns. This saves landfill space.

## Environmental Impact
The manufacturing of smartphones generates substantial emissions of volatile compounds annually.

## Disposal Methods
1. remove any non-recyclable parts before disposal.
2. place in appropriate bin.
3. Remember to research special recycling programs.

# Sustainability Information for batteries

## Recycling Guidelines
When recycling batteries, always sort by material type. Avoid including non-recyclable parts as it disrupts recycling processes.

## Creative Reuse
Turn old batteries into decorative items through folding techniques. Perfect for home decoration.

## Environmental Impact
Global batteries waste could accelerate climate change by our children's lifetime if current trends continue.

## Disposal Methods
1. remove any non-recyclable parts before disposal.
2. use specialized recycling service.
3. Remember to check local regulations.

# Sustainability Information for televisions

## Recycling Guidelines
Most recycling facilities process televisions by pulping and reforming, requiring removal of contaminants.

## Creative Reuse
televisions can be repurposed into garden planters by painting and decorating. This saves carbon emissions.

## Environmental Impact
The average person disposes of several tons of televisions yearly, contributing to landfill overflow.

## Disposal Methods
1. rinse thoroughly before disposal.
2. take to collection center.
3. Remember to avoid contamination with food.
