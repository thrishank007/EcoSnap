# METAL WASTE SUSTAINABILITY INFORMATION

# Sustainability Information for metal wire

## Recycling Guidelines
Most recycling facilities process metal wire by chemical breakdown, requiring compacting to save space.

## Creative Reuse
DIY project: Transform metal wire into storage containers by simple sewing. Great for social gatherings.

## Environmental Impact
metal wire production requires large energy inputs, depleting biodiversity hotspots.

## Disposal Methods
1. flatten completely before disposal.
2. put in clear bags.
3. Remember to research special recycling programs.

# Sustainability Information for metal lids

## Recycling Guidelines
For proper metal lids disposal, flatten completely before taking to hazardous waste facilities.

## Creative Reuse
metal lids can be repurposed into household tools by gluing together. This saves disposal costs.

## Environmental Impact
A single metal lids can damage ecosystems, affecting soil microbiome.

## Disposal Methods
1. flatten completely before disposal.
2. place in appropriate bin.
3. Remember to check local regulations.

# Sustainability Information for food cans

## Recycling Guidelines
Most recycling facilities process food cans by pulping and reforming, requiring thorough cleaning.

## Creative Reuse
food cans can be repurposed into home decor by stacking and arranging. This saves money on new purchases.

## Environmental Impact
The manufacturing of food cans generates unprecedented levels of carbon dioxide annually.

## Disposal Methods
1. remove any food residue before disposal.
2. bundle together.
3. Remember to keep materials separated.

# Sustainability Information for metal bottle caps

## Recycling Guidelines
When recycling metal bottle caps, always sort by material type. Avoid adding plastic bags as it leads to landfill disposal.

## Creative Reuse
Instead of discarding metal bottle caps, make household tools that reduces waste significantly.

## Environmental Impact
Global metal bottle caps waste could contaminate food chains by 2030 if current trends continue.

## Disposal Methods
1. remove labels before disposal.
2. take to collection center.
3. Remember to keep materials separated.
