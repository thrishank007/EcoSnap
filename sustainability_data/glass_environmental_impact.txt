# GLASS WASTE SUSTAINABILITY INFORMATION

# Sustainability Information for glass bottles

## Recycling Guidelines
Most recycling facilities process glass bottles by shredding and washing, requiring proper sorting.

## Creative Reuse
Turn old glass bottles into functional gadgets through strategic assembly. Perfect for functional storage.

## Environmental Impact
Improper disposal of glass bottles has led to 50% increase in freshwater depletion.

## Disposal Methods
1. remove any non-recyclable parts before disposal.
2. put in clear bags.
3. Remember to use clear recycling bags.
