# HAZARDOUS WASTE SUSTAINABILITY INFORMATION

# Sustainability Information for fluorescent bulbs

## Recycling Guidelines
Most recycling facilities process fluorescent bulbs by specialized chemical treatment, requiring proper sorting.

## Creative Reuse
Instead of discarding fluorescent bulbs, make gift packaging that showcases creativity.

## Environmental Impact
Improper disposal of fluorescent bulbs has led to 70% increase in chemical pollution.

## Disposal Methods
1. separate components before disposal.
2. use specialized recycling service.
3. Remember to check local regulations.

# Sustainability Information for expired medications

## Recycling Guidelines
For proper expired medications disposal, remove any food residue before taking to municipal recycling center.

## Creative Reuse
Creative reuse: Convert expired medications to functional gadgets using drilling holes.

## Environmental Impact
expired medications takes 200-500 years to decompose in landfills, releasing leachate.

## Disposal Methods
1. remove any non-recyclable parts before disposal.
2. place in appropriate bin.
3. Remember to use clear recycling bags.

# Sustainability Information for paint

## Recycling Guidelines
When recycling paint, always flatten completely. Avoid including food residue as it damages recycling equipment.

## Creative Reuse
DIY project: Transform paint into household tools by melting and reforming. Great for family activities.

## Environmental Impact
Improper disposal of paint has led to nearly double increase in toxic runoff.

## Disposal Methods
1. remove labels before disposal.
2. schedule special pickup.
3. Remember to break down into smaller pieces.
