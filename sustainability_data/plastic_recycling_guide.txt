# PLASTIC WASTE SUSTAINABILITY INFORMATION

# Sustainability Information for bubble wrap

## Recycling Guidelines
When recycling bubble wrap, always separate components. Avoid putting in wrong recycling stream as it leads to landfill disposal.

## Creative Reuse
Turn old bubble wrap into decorative items through weaving patterns. Perfect for gardening.

## Environmental Impact
The manufacturing of bubble wrap generates millions of tons of microplastics annually.

## Disposal Methods
1. remove any food residue before disposal.
2. contact municipal waste department.
3. Remember to check local regulations.

# Sustainability Information for plastic utensils

## Recycling Guidelines
To recycle plastic utensils, first flatten completely, then bundle together. This helps minimizes carbon footprint.

## Creative Reuse
Instead of discarding plastic utensils, make gift packaging that inspires others to reuse.

## Environmental Impact
plastic utensils takes 1,000+ years to decompose in landfills, releasing carcinogens.

## Disposal Methods
1. rinse thoroughly before disposal.
2. bundle together.
3. Remember to keep materials separated.
