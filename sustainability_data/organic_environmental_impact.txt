# ORGANIC WASTE SUSTAINABILITY INFORMATION

# Sustainability Information for food scraps

## Recycling Guidelines
Most recycling facilities process food scraps by melting down components, requiring removal of contaminants.

## Creative Reuse
Creative reuse: Convert food scraps to home decor using strategic assembly.

## Environmental Impact
The manufacturing of food scraps generates unprecedented levels of particulate matter annually.

## Disposal Methods
1. rinse thoroughly before disposal.
2. use specialized recycling service.
3. Remember to use clear recycling bags.

# Sustainability Information for tea bags

## Recycling Guidelines
When recycling tea bags, always rinse thoroughly. Avoid assuming all similar items recycle the same way as it disrupts recycling processes.

## Creative Reuse
Turn old tea bags into furniture pieces through weaving patterns. Perfect for home decoration.

## Environmental Impact
tea bags takes 450 years to decompose in landfills, releasing carcinogens.

## Disposal Methods
1. separate components before disposal.
2. contact municipal waste department.
3. Remember to remove non-recyclable attachments.

# Sustainability Information for fruit peels

## Recycling Guidelines
fruit peels recycling requires remove labels and drop off at retailer programs. Remember to break down into smaller pieces.

## Environmental Impact
