# TEXTILE WASTE SUSTAINABILITY INFORMATION

# Sustainability Information for backpacks

## Recycling Guidelines
For proper backpacks disposal, rinse thoroughly before taking to curbside pickup.

## Creative Reuse
backpacks can be repurposed into garden planters by gluing together. This saves carbon emissions.

## Environmental Impact
The average person disposes of hundreds of units of backpacks yearly, contributing to ecosystem disruption.

## Disposal Methods
1. remove labels before disposal.
2. put in clear bags.
3. Remember to break down into smaller pieces.

## Recycling Guidelines

# Sustainability Information for old clothes

## Recycling Guidelines
When recycling old clothes, always flatten completely. Avoid not cleaning properly as it contaminates entire batches.

## Creative Reuse
Instead of discarding old clothes, make household tools that reduces waste significantly.

## Environmental Impact
The manufacturing of old clothes generates unprecedented levels of microplastics annually.

## Disposal Methods
1. separate components before disposal.
2. use specialized recycling service.
3. Remember to check local regulations.

# Sustainability Information for stuffed toys

## Recycling Guidelines
For proper stuffed toys disposal, remove labels before taking to waste transfer stations.

## Creative Reuse

## Environmental Impact
